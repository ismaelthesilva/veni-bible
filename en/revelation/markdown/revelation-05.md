# Revelation 5

### The sealed book

<sup>1</sup>And I saw in the right hand of Him who is seated on the throne a book written inside and on the back, sealed with seven seals.[^1]

<sup>2</sup>And I saw a strong angel proclaiming with a loud voice: Who is worthy to open the book and to break its seals?[^2] <sup>3</sup>But no one in heaven, or on the earth, or under the earth was able to open the book or to look at it. <sup>4</sup>I was weeping greatly, because no one was found worthy to open the book or to look at it.[^4] <sup>5</sup>Then one of the elders says to me: Do not weep; behold, the Lion of the tribe of Judah, the Root of David, has conquered, to open the book and its seven seals.[^5]

### Worthy is the Lamb

<sup>6</sup>And I saw, in the midst of the throne and of the four living creatures, and in the midst of the elders, a Lamb standing, as though slain, having seven horns and seven eyes, which are the Seven Spirits of God, sent out into all the earth.[^6] <sup>7</sup>He came and took it from the right hand of Him who is seated on the throne.[^7] <sup>8</sup>When He had taken the book, the four living creatures and the twenty-four elders fell down before the Lamb, each holding a harp and golden bowls full of incense, which are the prayers of the saints.[^8] <sup>9</sup>They sing a new song, saying:

&emsp;Worthy are You to take the book<br>
&emsp;and to open its seals,<br>
&emsp;for You were slain,<br>
&emsp;and by Your blood You purchased for God<br>
&emsp;those from every tribe, and tongue, and people, and nation;[^9]<br>
&emsp;<sup>10</sup>and You made them, for our God,<br>
&emsp;a kingdom and priests;<br>
&emsp;and they will reign on the earth.[^10]

<sup>11</sup>And I saw, and I heard the voice of many angels around the throne and the living creatures and the elders; and their number was myriads of myriads and thousands of thousands,[^11] <sup>12</sup>saying with a loud voice:

&emsp;Worthy is the slain Lamb<br>
&emsp;to receive the power, and riches, and wisdom,<br>
&emsp;and strength, and honor, and glory, and blessing.[^12]

<sup>13</sup>And every creature that is in heaven, and on the earth, and under the earth, and on the sea, and all things that are in them, I heard saying:

&emsp;To Him who is seated on the throne and to the Lamb<br>
&emsp;be the blessing, and the honor, and the glory, and the dominion,<br>
&emsp;forever and ever.[^13]

<sup>14</sup>The four living creatures were saying: Amen. And the elders fell down and worshiped.[^14]

---

[^1]: **5:1** “In the right hand”: Gr. ἐπὶ τὴν δεξιάν, lit. “on the right hand”. “Book”: Gr. βιβλίον, a scroll written on both sides and sealed along its edge, so that each seal opened in turn; “book” is kept throughout for the one Greek word (cf. 1:11; 6:14; 22:7). “On the back”: Gr. ὄπισθεν, lit. “behind”; cf. Ezek 2:9–10. “Sealed with seven seals”: cf. Isa 29:11; Dan 12:4. Variant: the Byzantine text reads “outside” (ἔξωθεν).

[^2]: **5:2** “Strong”: Gr. ἰσχυρός (cf. ἰσχύς, “strength”, 5:12). “Break”: Gr. λῦσαι, lit. “loose”, the same verb as in 1:5 (“freed”).

[^4]: **5:4** Variant: the Textus Receptus reads “to open and to read the book”.

[^5]: **5:5** “The Lion of the tribe of Judah”: cf. Gen 49:9–10. “The Root of David”: cf. Isa 11:1, 10; Rev 22:16. “Has conquered”: Gr. νικάω (cf. 3:21). Variant: the Byzantine text reads “who opens the book”; the Textus Receptus, “to open the book and to loose its seven seals”.

[^6]: **5:6** “Lamb”: Gr. ἀρνίον, a title of Christ in the book (except 13:11). “Slain”: Gr. σφάζω, to slaughter in sacrifice (cf. Isa 53:7, Septuagint σφαγή; Jer 11:19). “Horns”: a symbol of power (cf. Deut 33:17; Ps 89:17). “Seven eyes… sent out into all the earth”: cf. Zech 4:10.

[^7]: **5:7** “Took it”: a dramatic perfect (εἴληφεν), rendered as a simple past; the object, the book, is implied. The Textus Receptus makes it explicit (“took the book”).

[^8]: **5:8** “Fell down”: Gr. πίπτω (cf. 4:10). “Harp”: Gr. κιθάρα, a stringed instrument like the lyre (cf. 14:2; 15:2). “Bowls”: Gr. φιάλαι, the same word as the bowls of wrath (15:7; 16:1). “Prayers of the saints”: cf. Ps 141:2; Rev 8:3–4.

[^9]: **5:9** “New song”: cf. Ps 96:1; Isa 42:10; Rev 14:3. “Purchased”: Gr. ἀγοράζω (cf. 14:3–4). “Those from”: the Greek says only “from” (ἐκ), with no expressed object. Variant: Codex Sinaiticus, the Byzantine text, most manuscripts and the Textus Receptus read “purchased us” (ἡμᾶς); the SBLGNT follows Codex Alexandrinus, which omits it. “Tribe, and tongue, and people, and nation”: a formula repeated in the book (cf. 7:9; 14:6).

[^10]: **5:10** Cf. 1:6; Ex 19:6. “They will reign”: follows the reading βασιλεύσουσιν, of Codex Sinaiticus and other manuscripts (also in the Robinson-Pierpont Byzantine text and in Nestle 1904); the SBLGNT follows Codex Alexandrinus, which reads βασιλεύουσιν, “they reign”. Cf. 20:4, 6; 22:5. Variant: the Byzantine text reads “kings and priests”; the Textus Receptus, “made us kings and priests… and we will reign”.

[^11]: **5:11** “Myriads of myriads and thousands of thousands”: cf. Dan 7:10.

[^12]: **5:12** “Slain Lamb”: a perfect participle (ἐσφαγμένον); the Lamb remains the one who was slain. Seven attributes, governed by a single article in the SBLGNT. “Blessing”: Gr. εὐλογία (cf. 5:13; 7:12).

[^13]: **5:13** “Creature”: Gr. κτίσμα (cf. κτίζω, 4:11). “Dominion”: Gr. κράτος (cf. 1:6).

[^14]: **5:14** Variant: the Textus Receptus reads “the twenty-four elders… and worshiped Him who lives forever and ever”.
