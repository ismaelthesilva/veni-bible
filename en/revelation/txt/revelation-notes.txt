Revelation 1:1	Gr. δούλοις, lit. “slaves”. “Made it known by signs”: Gr. ἐσήμανεν, a verb related to σημεῖον, “sign”; the book communicates through symbols.
Revelation 1:2	The μαρτυρ- family is always rendered testify / testimony / Witness (cf. 12:17; 19:10).
Revelation 1:3	A single “blessed” (μακάριος) covers the reader, in the singular (the one who reads aloud in the assembly), and a single group that hears and keeps; one article governs both participles. τηρέω = “keep” throughout the book (cf. 12:17; 14:12).
Revelation 1:4	Gr. ἀπὸ ὁ ὤν: the divine name stays in the nominative after the preposition, as an indeclinable name (cf. Ex 3:14). “Who Is Coming”: present tense (ὁ ἐρχόμενος), the same verb as “He is coming” in 1:7. “Seven Spirits”: the capital follows their place in the greeting, between Him Who Is and Jesus Christ, as the source of grace and peace; alternative reading: seven angelic spirits (cf. 8:2).
Revelation 1:5	Variants: the Textus Receptus reads “loved us”; it and the Byzantine text read “washed us” (λούσαντι). The SBLGNT is followed, with Papyrus 18 and codices Sinaiticus, Alexandrinus and Ephraemi: “loves us” (ἀγαπῶντι) and “freed us” (λύσαντι).
Revelation 1:6	Variant: the Textus Receptus reads “kings and priests”; the SBLGNT, “a kingdom, priests” (cf. Ex 19:6). “Forever and ever” (εἰς τοὺς αἰῶνας τῶν αἰώνων, lit. “to the ages of the ages”): the formula is rendered the same way throughout the book.
Revelation 1:7	Cf. Dan 7:13; Zech 12:10. “Yes, amen”: Gr. ναί (Greek) + ἀμήν (Hebrew), a double affirmation.
Revelation 1:8	“Almighty”: Gr. παντοκράτωρ, which in the Septuagint usually renders “LORD of Hosts” (e.g. Amos 4:13) and, in Job, “Shaddai”. Variant: the Textus Receptus adds “the Beginning and the End” and reads “says the Lord”, without “God”.
Revelation 1:9	“Was” renders ἐγενόμην, a verb repeated in 1:9, 1:10 and 1:18. ὑπομονή = “perseverance” (cf. 14:12). Variant: the Byzantine text reads “in Christ Jesus”, and the Textus Receptus “of Jesus Christ”; both read “the Testimony of Jesus Christ”.
Revelation 1:10	Gr. ἐν τῇ κυριακῇ ἡμέρᾳ, with the adjective κυριακός, “belonging to the Lord”; the text does not name the day. Cf. Ex 20:10; Isa 58:13; Mark 2:28.
Revelation 1:11	Gr. βιβλίον, a scroll; “book” is kept for consistency with 5:1ff. Variant: the Textus Receptus begins the speech with “I am the Alpha and the Omega, the First and the Last” and adds “that are in Asia”.
Revelation 1:12	Gr. λυχνία; cf. the lampstand of the sanctuary, Ex 25:31–37.
Revelation 1:13	Cf. Dan 7:13; “a Son of Man”: Gr. υἱὸν ἀνθρώπου, without the article, as in Daniel. “Robe reaching to the feet”: Gr. ποδήρη, the term for the high priest’s garments in the Septuagint (Ex 28:4; 28:31 [LXX 28:27]; cf. Zech 3:4). The capital follows the self-identification in 1:17–18. Variant: the Byzantine text and the Textus Receptus read “the seven lampstands”.
Revelation 1:14	Cf. Dan 7:9 (the Ancient of Days).
Revelation 1:15	Gr. χαλκολίβανον, a metal of uncertain identification. “Refined”: the same verb as in 3:18 (πυρόω). Cf. Ezek 43:2.
Revelation 1:16	Cf. Isa 49:2; Judg 5:31.
Revelation 1:17	“Right hand”: Gr. τὴν δεξιάν, “the right”, without the word “hand” (also in 1:20); in 1:16 the Greek has “right hand”. Variant: the Textus Receptus adds “hand” here. Cf. Isa 44:6; 48:12.
Revelation 1:18	“The grave”: Gr. ᾅδης (Hades), equivalent to Heb. sheol (cf. Hos 13:14). Variant: the Byzantine text and the Textus Receptus add “Amen” after “forever and ever”; the Textus Receptus reads “of the grave and of death”.
Revelation 1:19	Or: “the things that you saw, that is, the things that are and the things that are about to happen after these things”.
Revelation 1:20	Gr. ἄγγελοι, or “messengers”.
Revelation 2:1	“Angel”: Gr. ἄγγελος, or “messenger” (cf. 1:20). “Thus says”: Gr. τάδε λέγει, the prophetic formula of the Septuagint (“Thus says the LORD”), repeated in all seven letters. “Holds”: Gr. κρατῶν, from κρατέω, “to hold firmly”.
Revelation 2:2	“Toil”: Gr. κόπος, “wearying toil”; the related verb appears in 2:3 (“have not grown weary”).
Revelation 2:5	Gr. κινήσω, “move, remove from its place”; the lampstand is the church (1:20). “Have fallen”: perfect (πέπτωκας), a fall whose state remains. Variant: the Byzantine text and the Textus Receptus add “quickly” after “I am coming to you”.
Revelation 2:6	The Nicolaitans are mentioned only in Revelation (cf. 2:15).
Revelation 2:7	“To the one who conquers” (νικῶντι): νικάω = “conquer” in all seven letters. Gr. ξύλον, “wood, tree”, the term of Gen 2:9 (Septuagint), also used in the New Testament for the cross (Acts 5:30); cf. 22:2, 14. Variant: the Byzantine text reads “the paradise of My God”; the Textus Receptus, “in the midst of the paradise of God”.
Revelation 2:8	“Was dead”: Gr. ἐγένετο νεκρός, as in 1:18 (“I was dead”). “Came to life”: Gr. ἔζησεν, the same verb as in 20:4–5.
Revelation 2:9	Or “slander” (Gr. βλασφημία). The expression does not refer to the Jewish people as such, but to those who, according to the text itself, “say they are Jews, and are not” (cf. 3:9; Rom 2:28–29). Variant: the Byzantine text and the Textus Receptus begin “I know your works, and your tribulation”.
Revelation 2:10	“Tested”: Gr. πειρασθῆτε, the same verb as in 2:2 (“you have tested”). “Ten days”: cf. Dan 1:12, 14. “Crown”: Gr. στέφανος, the victor’s wreath, distinct from διάδημα, “diadem” (12:3; 13:1; 19:12).
Revelation 2:11	Gr. οὐ μή, an emphatic negation. Second death: cf. 20:6, 14; 21:8.
Revelation 2:13	“Hold fast”: Gr. κρατέω, the same verb as in 2:1 (“holds”), 2:14–15 (“hold”) and 2:25 (“hold fast”). “My faith”: or “faith in Me” (cf. 14:12). “Witness”: Gr. μάρτυς, the same word as in 1:5, which later gave “martyr”. “Dwell… dwells”: the same verb (κατοικέω). Variant: the Byzantine text and the Textus Receptus begin “I know your works and where you dwell”.
Revelation 2:14	Cf. Num 25:1–3; 31:16. “Teaching… taught”: Gr. διδαχή / ἐδίδασκεν. “Stumbling block”: Gr. σκάνδαλον. “Prostitute themselves”: Gr. πορνεύω; in Num 25:1 the Hebrew uses זָנָה‎, “to prostitute oneself”, linked to idolatry; in Revelation the term joins sexual and spiritual unfaithfulness (cf. 17:1–2).
Revelation 2:15	Variant: the Textus Receptus reads “which I hate” in place of “likewise”.
Revelation 2:16	“Soon”: Gr. ταχύ (cf. 3:11; 22:7, 12, 20). “Make war”: Gr. πολεμέω (cf. 12:7; 17:14; 19:11).
Revelation 2:17	Hidden manna: cf. Ex 16:33–34. “Pebble”: Gr. ψῆφος, a small stone used in voting and as a token of admission. “New”: Gr. καινός (cf. 3:12; 21:1, 5). “Knows”: the same verb as “I know” (οἶδα).
Revelation 2:18	Cf. 1:14–15.
Revelation 2:19	Or: “I know your works, that is, your love, and faith…”.
Revelation 2:20	“Tolerate”: Gr. ἀφεῖς, the same verb as in 2:4 (“have left”). “Woman”: Gr. γυνή, also “wife”; variant: the Byzantine text reads “your wife Jezebel”, and the Textus Receptus “I have a few things against you”. “Deceives”: Gr. πλανάω (cf. 12:9; 13:14; 20:3, 8, 10). Cf. 1 Kgs 16:31.
Revelation 2:22	“Bed”: Gr. κλίνη, also a sickbed. “Great tribulation”: cf. 7:14. Variant: the Textus Receptus reads “of their works”.
Revelation 2:23	“With pestilence”: Gr. ἐν θανάτῳ, lit. “with death”; in the Septuagint θάνατος often renders Heb. דֶּבֶר‎, “pestilence” (cf. 6:8; 18:8; Ezek 5:12; 33:27). “Minds”: Gr. νεφροί, lit. “kidneys”, in Hebrew thought the seat of the emotions and conscience (Jer 17:10, Heb. כְּלָיוֹת‎).
Revelation 2:24	“Remnant”: Gr. λοιποί, “those who remain”; the same word as in 12:17. Where it designates other groups (9:20; 19:21; 20:5), it is rendered “the rest”. “Burden”: Gr. βάρος, cf. Acts 15:28–29. “Depths”: cf. 1 Cor 2:10.
Revelation 2:26	“Keeps”: Gr. τηρέω (cf. 1:3). “Nations”: Gr. ἔθνη.
Revelation 2:27	Cf. Ps 2:8–9. “Shepherd”: Gr. ποιμαίνω, “to tend as a shepherd”, as in the Septuagint of Ps 2:9; the Hebrew reads “you will break” (תְּרֹעֵם‎). Cf. 7:17; 12:5; 19:15.
Revelation 2:28	Cf. 22:16.
Revelation 3:1	“Seven Spirits”: cf. 1:4. “A name of being alive”: Gr. ὄνομα ἔχεις ὅτι ζῇς, lit. “a name that you live”; “name”, that is, reputation; the word recurs in 3:4–5.
Revelation 3:2	“Be watchful”: Gr. γίνου γρηγορῶν, like “Be faithful” (2:10); γρηγορέω = “watch” (3:3; 16:15). “The things that remain”: Gr. τὰ λοιπά (plural). Variant: the Byzantine text reads “which you were about to throw away” (ἔμελλες ἀποβάλλειν); the Textus Receptus reads “which is about to die” (μέλλει, singular and present) and “before God”, without “My”.
Revelation 3:3	“Like a thief”: cf. 16:15; Matt 24:43; 1 Thess 5:2. “I will come”: Gr. ἥξω, as in 2:25 (“until I come”). “By no means”: Gr. οὐ μή, an emphatic negation. Variant: the Byzantine text and the Textus Receptus read “I will come upon you like a thief”.
Revelation 3:4	“Names”: that is, persons (cf. Acts 1:15); a wordplay with “name” in 3:1 and 3:5. “Garments”: Gr. ἱμάτια (cf. 3:5, 18; 16:15). “Defiled”: Gr. μολύνω (cf. 14:4).
Revelation 3:5	“Book of life”: cf. Ex 32:32–33; Dan 12:1; Phil 4:3; Rev 13:8; 20:12, 15. “Blot out”: Gr. ἐξαλείφω, the same verb as in 7:17 and 21:4 (“wipe away”). Cf. Matt 10:32. Variant: the Byzantine text and the Textus Receptus read “this one will be clothed” (οὗτος) in place of “will thus be clothed” (οὕτως).
Revelation 3:7	Cf. Isa 22:22. “True One”: Gr. ἀληθινός (cf. 3:14; 19:11). Variant: the Textus Receptus reads “and no one shuts, and shuts, and no one opens” (κλείει, present, in both places); the Byzantine text, “and no one will shut it except the one who opens, and no one will open”.
Revelation 3:8	“Set”: Gr. δέδωκα, lit. “I have given”. “That you have”: or “because you have”. “Power”: Gr. δύναμις (cf. 4:11; 5:12).
Revelation 3:9	“Handing over”: Gr. διδῶ, lit. “I give”; the Greek sentence breaks off and starts again (“behold, I will make them…”). “Bow down”: Gr. προσκυνέω, the verb rendered “worship” when the object is God (cf. 4:10; 14:7); here, homage (cf. Isa 60:14 Heb.; 45:14; 49:23). On “synagogue of Satan”, cf. 2:9.
Revelation 3:10	“Kept… keep”: the same verb (τηρέω). “The word of My perseverance”: or “My word about perseverance”. “Testing… test”: Gr. πειρασμός / πειράζω (cf. πειράζω in 2:2, 10). “The whole world”: Gr. οἰκουμένη ὅλη, lit. “the whole inhabited world” (cf. 12:9). “Those who dwell on the earth”: a recurring formula (cf. 6:10; 13:8).
Revelation 3:11	“Soon”: Gr. ταχύ (cf. 2:16). “Hold fast”: Gr. κρατέω (cf. 2:25). “Crown”: Gr. στέφανος (cf. 2:10). Variant: the Textus Receptus begins “Behold, I am coming soon”.
Revelation 3:12	“Sanctuary”: Gr. ναός, the inner part of the temple, distinct from ἱερόν, the whole temple complex; in Revelation, always ναός (cf. 7:15; 11:19; 15:5). “New Jerusalem”: cf. 21:2. “New name”: cf. 2:17; Isa 62:2.
Revelation 3:14	“The Amen”: cf. Isa 65:16 (Heb. “the God of amen”; Septuagint “the true God”). “Beginning”: Gr. ἀρχή, “beginning, origin” or “ruler”; the same term used as a title in 21:6 and 22:13 (“the Beginning and the End”). Cf. John 1:3; Col 1:15–18.
Revelation 3:15	“Hot”: Gr. ζεστός, lit. “boiling”.
Revelation 3:16	“Am about to”: Gr. μέλλω (cf. 1:19; 2:10).
Revelation 3:17	“Have grown rich”: perfect (πεπλούτηκα), “I have become rich” (a state that remains).
Revelation 3:18	“Refined”: Gr. πυρόω (cf. 1:15). “Clothe yourself”: Gr. περιβάλλω, the same verb as in 3:5 (“clothed”).
Revelation 3:19	“Love”: Gr. φιλέω. Cf. Prov 3:12 (Septuagint, with the same verb “rebuke”).
Revelation 3:20	“Stand”: perfect (ἕστηκα), “I have taken My stand and remain”. “I will come in”: the Greek has “and I will come in” (καί introducing the consequence, in the Hebrew manner). “Dine”: Gr. δειπνέω; cf. δεῖπνον, the wedding supper (19:9).
Revelation 3:21	“Grant”: Gr. δώσω, lit. “I will give”. Cf. 2:26–27; 22:1.
Revelation 4:1	“After these things”: Gr. μετὰ ταῦτα, as in 1:19; “the things that must happen” takes up 1:1. The SBLGNT punctuates the second “after these things” as the start of 4:2 (“After these things, immediately I was in the Spirit”); the punctuation followed here is that of the Byzantine text and the Textus Receptus (without their “and” at the start of 4:2). “Like a trumpet”: cf. 1:10; Ex 19:16–20.
Revelation 4:2	“I was”: Gr. ἐγενόμην, as in 1:10. “One seated”: cf. 1 Kgs 22:19; Isa 6:1; Ezek 1:26; Dan 7:9.
Revelation 4:3	“Jasper… sardius”: Gr. ἴασπις and σάρδιον, stones of uncertain identification (cf. 21:11, 19–20). “Rainbow”: cf. Gen 9:13; Ezek 1:28. Variant: the Byzantine text omits “and He who was seated”.
Revelation 4:4	“Elders”: Gr. πρεσβύτεροι. “Clothed”: Gr. περιβάλλω, lit. “to throw around”, as in 3:5. “Crowns”: Gr. στέφανος (cf. 2:10).
Revelation 4:5	“Voices”: Gr. φωναί, also “rumblings” (cf. Ex 19:16, Heb. קֹלֹת‎; Rev 8:5; 11:19; 16:18). “Torches”: Gr. λαμπάδες (cf. 8:10; Ezek 1:13; cf. the seven lamps of Zech 4:2). “Seven Spirits”: cf. 1:4.
Revelation 4:6	“Sea of glass”: cf. 15:2; Ezek 1:22. “Living creatures”: Gr. ζῷα, lit. “living beings”; cf. Ezek 1:5, Heb. חַיּוֹת‎.
Revelation 4:7	Cf. Ezek 1:10. “Young bull”: Gr. μόσχος.
Revelation 4:8	Cf. Isa 6:2–3. “He Who Was, and Who Is, and Who Is Coming”: the formula of 1:4, 8, here with “Was” first. “They have no rest, day and night”: the same expression as in 14:11.
Revelation 4:9	“Whenever… give”: Gr. ὅταν with the future, a repeated action. “Him who lives forever and ever”: cf. 1:18; 10:6; 15:7; Dan 4:34 (Aram. 4:31); 12:7.
Revelation 4:10	“Will fall down”: Gr. πίπτω, lit. “fall”; before God, prostration in worship (cf. 1:17). “Will worship”: Gr. προσκυνέω, “worship” because the object is God (contrast 3:9, “bow down”). “Will cast”: Gr. βάλλω.
Revelation 4:11	“Created”: Gr. κτίζω (cf. “creation”, 3:14). “Because of Your will”: Gr. διὰ τὸ θέλημά σου. Variant: the Textus Receptus reads “Worthy are You, O Lord” (without “and our God”) and “they are and were created”; the Byzantine text adds “the Holy One” after “our Lord and God”.
Revelation 5:1	“In the right hand”: Gr. ἐπὶ τὴν δεξιάν, lit. “on the right hand”. “Book”: Gr. βιβλίον, a scroll written on both sides and sealed along its edge, so that each seal opened in turn; “book” is kept throughout for the one Greek word (cf. 1:11; 6:14; 22:7). “On the back”: Gr. ὄπισθεν, lit. “behind”; cf. Ezek 2:9–10. “Sealed with seven seals”: cf. Isa 29:11; Dan 12:4. Variant: the Byzantine text reads “outside” (ἔξωθεν).
Revelation 5:2	“Strong”: Gr. ἰσχυρός (cf. ἰσχύς, “strength”, 5:12). “Break”: Gr. λῦσαι, lit. “loose”, the same verb as in 1:5 (“freed”).
Revelation 5:4	Variant: the Textus Receptus reads “to open and to read the book”.
Revelation 5:5	“The Lion of the tribe of Judah”: cf. Gen 49:9–10. “The Root of David”: cf. Isa 11:1, 10; Rev 22:16. “Has conquered”: Gr. νικάω (cf. 3:21). Variant: the Byzantine text reads “who opens the book”; the Textus Receptus, “to open the book and to loose its seven seals”.
Revelation 5:6	“Lamb”: Gr. ἀρνίον, a title of Christ in the book (except 13:11). “Slain”: Gr. σφάζω, to slaughter in sacrifice (cf. Isa 53:7, Septuagint σφαγή; Jer 11:19). “Horns”: a symbol of power (cf. Deut 33:17; Ps 89:17). “Seven eyes… sent out into all the earth”: cf. Zech 4:10.
Revelation 5:7	“Took it”: a dramatic perfect (εἴληφεν), rendered as a simple past; the object, the book, is implied. The Textus Receptus makes it explicit (“took the book”).
Revelation 5:8	“Fell down”: Gr. πίπτω (cf. 4:10). “Harp”: Gr. κιθάρα, a stringed instrument like the lyre (cf. 14:2; 15:2). “Bowls”: Gr. φιάλαι, the same word as the bowls of wrath (15:7; 16:1). “Prayers of the saints”: cf. Ps 141:2; Rev 8:3–4.
Revelation 5:9	“New song”: cf. Ps 96:1; Isa 42:10; Rev 14:3. “Purchased”: Gr. ἀγοράζω (cf. 14:3–4). “Those from”: the Greek says only “from” (ἐκ), with no expressed object. Variant: Codex Sinaiticus, the Byzantine text, most manuscripts and the Textus Receptus read “purchased us” (ἡμᾶς); the SBLGNT follows Codex Alexandrinus, which omits it. “Tribe, and tongue, and people, and nation”: a formula repeated in the book (cf. 7:9; 14:6).
Revelation 5:10	Cf. 1:6; Ex 19:6. “They will reign”: follows the reading βασιλεύσουσιν, of Codex Sinaiticus and other manuscripts (also in the Robinson-Pierpont Byzantine text and in Nestle 1904); the SBLGNT follows Codex Alexandrinus, which reads βασιλεύουσιν, “they reign”. Cf. 20:4, 6; 22:5. Variant: the Byzantine text reads “kings and priests”; the Textus Receptus, “made us kings and priests… and we will reign”.
Revelation 5:11	“Myriads of myriads and thousands of thousands”: cf. Dan 7:10.
Revelation 5:12	“Slain Lamb”: a perfect participle (ἐσφαγμένον); the Lamb remains the one who was slain. Seven attributes, governed by a single article in the SBLGNT. “Blessing”: Gr. εὐλογία (cf. 5:13; 7:12).
Revelation 5:13	“Creature”: Gr. κτίσμα (cf. κτίζω, 4:11). “Dominion”: Gr. κράτος (cf. 1:6).
Revelation 5:14	Variant: the Textus Receptus reads “the twenty-four elders… and worshiped Him who lives forever and ever”.
Revelation 6:1	“Come”: Gr. ἔρχου. Variant: the Textus Receptus adds “and see” (καὶ βλέπε) in 6:1, 3, 5 and 7; Codex Sinaiticus and the Byzantine text have a similar addition (καὶ ἴδε); in the Byzantine text, in 6:1, 5 and 7.
Revelation 6:2	“He who sat”: the capital identifies the rider with the rider of the white horse in 19:11–16; in the book, “white”, “crown” and “conquer” are linked to Christ and His people (cf. 3:21; 5:5; 14:14). Another reading sees here a conquering power. “Bow”: Gr. τόξον. “Crown”: Gr. στέφανος, the victor’s crown (cf. 2:10; 3:11). “Conquering and to conquer”: Gr. νικῶν καὶ ἵνα νικήσῃ (cf. νικάω, 5:5). Horses of different colors: cf. Zech 1:8; 6:1–8.
Revelation 6:4	“Red”: Gr. πυρρός, lit. “fire-colored”. “Slay”: Gr. σφάζω, the same verb rendered “slain” in 5:6, 9, 12 and 6:9. “Sword”: Gr. μάχαιρα.
Revelation 6:5	“Balance”: Gr. ζυγός, lit. “yoke”, the beam of the scales.
Revelation 6:6	“Measure”: Gr. χοῖνιξ, a little more than a liter (about a quart). “Denarius”: a Roman silver coin (cf. Matt 20:2). “Do not harm”: Gr. ἀδικέω (cf. 2:11; 7:2–3; 9:4). Variant: the Byzantine text and the Textus Receptus read “a voice”, without “something like”.
Revelation 6:8	“Pale”: Gr. χλωρός, lit. “pale green” (cf. 8:7; 9:4, “green”). “The grave”: Gr. ᾅδης (cf. 1:18). “Sword”: here Gr. ῥομφαία (cf. 1:16). “Pestilence”: Gr. θάνατος, lit. “death”; the Septuagint uses the same word for “pestilence” in Ezek 14:21. Sword, famine, pestilence and wild beasts: cf. Ezek 14:21; Lev 26:22–26. Variant: the Byzantine text reads “was given to him” (singular); the Textus Receptus, like the SBLGNT, has the plural.
Revelation 6:9	“Altar”: Gr. θυσιαστήριον. “Souls”: Gr. ψυχαί, also “lives” (cf. Lev 4:7; 17:11). “Slain”: Gr. σφάζω (cf. 5:6). “Word of God… testimony”: cf. 1:2, 9. Variant: the Byzantine text reads “the testimony of the Lamb”.
Revelation 6:10	“Sovereign”: Gr. δεσπότης, “master, owner” (cf. Luke 2:29; Acts 4:24). “The Holy and True One”: cf. 3:7. “How long”: cf. Ps 79:5, 10; Zech 1:12. “Avenge… on”: Gr. ἐκδικέω… ἐκ, lit. “avenge… from” (cf. 19:2; Deut 32:43). “Those who dwell on the earth”: cf. 3:10.
Revelation 6:11	“Robe”: Gr. στολή, a long robe (cf. 7:9, 13–14; 22:14). “The number”: the Greek says only “until both their fellow servants and their brothers… were completed”. Variant: the Byzantine text reads “until they complete [their course]” (πληρώσωσιν). “Fellow servants”: Gr. σύνδουλοι (cf. 19:10; 22:9). Variant: the Textus Receptus reads “white robes were given to each”.
Revelation 6:12	“Earthquake”: Gr. σεισμός (cf. 8:5; 11:13, 19; 16:18). “Sackcloth of hair”: Gr. σάκκος τρίχινος, a dark cloth of hair, usually goat’s hair. Sun, moon and stars: cf. Joel 2:10, 31 (Heb. 3:4); Isa 13:10; Matt 24:29. Variant: the Textus Receptus reads “and behold, there was” and omits “whole”.
Revelation 6:13	“Casts”: Gr. βάλλω. “Unripe figs”: Gr. ὄλυνθοι, figs that fall before ripening. Cf. Isa 34:4.
Revelation 6:14	“Scroll”: Gr. βιβλίον, “book”, here the scroll that is rolled up (cf. 5:1; Isa 34:4). “Were moved”: plural in the Greek.
Revelation 6:15	“The great ones”: Gr. μεγιστᾶνες, the nobles. “Commanders”: Gr. χιλίαρχοι, lit. “commanders of a thousand”. “Slave”: Gr. δοῦλος, rendered “servant” in 1:1; here in contrast with “free” (cf. 13:16; 19:18). Cf. Isa 2:10, 19, 21.
Revelation 6:16	Cf. Hos 10:8; Luke 23:30. “Face”: Gr. πρόσωπον.
Revelation 6:17	“Their”: of Him who is seated on the throne and of the Lamb (6:16). Variant: Codex Alexandrinus, the Robinson-Pierpont Byzantine text and the Textus Receptus read “of His wrath”; the SBLGNT is followed, with Codex Sinaiticus and Ephraemi. “Stand”: Gr. σταθῆναι (cf. 5:6, the Lamb “standing”; Nah 1:6; Mal 3:2). “The great day”: cf. Joel 2:11; Zeph 1:14.
Revelation 7:1	“After this”: Gr. μετὰ τοῦτο (singular); in 7:9, μετὰ ταῦτα, “after these things” (cf. 4:1). Variant: the Byzantine text reads “And after this”; the Textus Receptus, “And after these things”. “Holding”: Gr. κρατέω (cf. 2:1). “Four corners… four winds”: cf. Isa 11:12; Jer 49:36; Dan 7:2; Zech 6:5.
Revelation 7:2	“From the rising of the sun”: that is, from the east. “The seal of the living God”: Gr. σφραγῖδα θεοῦ ζῶντος, lit. “a seal of the living God”. “Harm”: Gr. ἀδικέω (cf. 6:6).
Revelation 7:3	“Sealed”: Gr. σφραγίζω (cf. 10:4; 22:10). “Foreheads”: cf. 9:4; 14:1; 22:4; Ezek 9:4; Ex 28:36–38.
Revelation 7:5	The list begins with Judah (cf. 5:5), includes Levi and Joseph, and does not include Dan or Ephraim (cf. Gen 49; Ezek 48). Variant: the Textus Receptus repeats “sealed” after each tribe.
Revelation 7:9	“From every nation, and tribes, and peoples, and tongues”: the order and number of the Greek, with a single preposition (cf. 5:9). “Clothed”: Gr. περιβάλλω (cf. 3:5). “Robes”: Gr. στολαί (cf. 6:11). “Palm branches”: Gr. φοίνικες (cf. Lev 23:40; John 12:13).
Revelation 7:10	“Salvation to our God”: the Greek has no verb (cf. 5:13; 19:1). Variant: the Textus Receptus reads “crying out”.
Revelation 7:11	“Were standing”: Gr. ἵστημι (cf. 5:6; 6:17). “Fell down”: Gr. πίπτω (cf. 4:10; 5:14).
Revelation 7:12	Seven attributes, each with the article (in 5:12, a single article for all seven). “Thanksgiving”: Gr. εὐχαριστία (cf. 4:9).
Revelation 7:13	“Spoke up”: Gr. ἀπεκρίθη, lit. “answered”, with no previous question.
Revelation 7:14	“Said”: Gr. εἴρηκα, perfect. “My lord”: addressed to the elder; the Textus Receptus omits “my”. “Came out”: Gr. οἱ ἐρχόμενοι, present participle, lit. “those who are coming”; rendered in the past, in harmony with “washed… made white”. “The great tribulation”: Gr. θλῖψις (cf. 1:9; 2:22; Dan 12:1; Matt 24:21). “Washed… made white”: cf. 22:14; Gen 49:11; Isa 1:18; Dan 11:35; 12:10 (Heb.).
Revelation 7:15	“Serve”: Gr. λατρεύω, the service of worship. “Sanctuary”: Gr. ναός (cf. 3:12; 11:19). “Will spread His tabernacle”: Gr. σκηνόω, “to dwell in a tent” (cf. 13:6; 21:3; John 1:14; Ezek 37:27).
Revelation 7:16	Cf. Isa 49:10. “By any means”: Gr. οὐδὲ μή.
Revelation 7:17	“Will shepherd”: Gr. ποιμαίνω (cf. 2:27; Ps 23:1–2; Ezek 34:23). “To springs of the waters of life”: lit. “to springs of waters of life” (cf. Isa 49:10). Variants: the Byzantine text reads “shepherds… guides” (present); the Textus Receptus reads “to living springs of waters”. “Wipe away”: Gr. ἐξαλείφω (cf. 3:5; 21:4; Isa 25:8).
Revelation 8:1	Cf. Hab 2:20; Zeph 1:7; Zech 2:13 (Heb. 2:17).
Revelation 8:2	“Stand”: Gr. ἑστήκασιν, perfect. “Trumpets”: Gr. σάλπιγγες (cf. 1:10; 4:1; Num 10:9; Josh 6:4; Joel 2:1).
Revelation 8:3	“At the altar”: Gr. ἐπὶ τοῦ θυσιαστηρίου, lit. “upon the altar”. “Censer”: Gr. λιβανωτός. “That he might add it to the prayers”: lit. “that he might give it to the prayers”. “Golden altar… before the throne”: cf. Ex 30:1–10; 40:5; Lev 16:12–13.
Revelation 8:4	“With the prayers”: Gr. ταῖς προσευχαῖς, the same dative as in 8:3, rendered there “to the prayers” (cf. 5:8; Ps 141:2).
Revelation 8:5	“Took”: Gr. εἴληφεν, perfect (cf. 5:7). Cf. Lev 16:12; Ezek 10:2. “Thunders, and voices, and flashes of lightning”: cf. 4:5; 11:19; 16:18; Ex 19:16.
Revelation 8:6	“Blow their trumpets”: Gr. σαλπίζω, “to sound the trumpet”.
Revelation 8:7	“Hail and fire”: cf. Ex 9:23–25. “They were cast”: Gr. ἐβλήθη, singular. “Green”: Gr. χλωρός, the same term rendered “pale” in 6:8. Variant: the Textus Receptus reads “the first angel” and omits “and a third of the earth was burned up”.
Revelation 8:8	“Something like a great mountain”: Gr. ὡς ὄρος μέγα. Cf. Jer 51:25. “Became blood”: cf. Ex 7:20–21. Variant: the Byzantine text omits “with fire”; the Textus Receptus has it.
Revelation 8:9	“Creatures”: Gr. κτίσματα (cf. 5:13). “That had life”: Gr. τὰ ἔχοντα ψυχάς, lit. “that had souls” (cf. 6:9; 16:3).
Revelation 8:10	“Torch”: Gr. λαμπάς (cf. 4:5).
Revelation 8:11	“The star is called Wormwood”: lit. “the name of the star is called: the Wormwood”. “Wormwood”: Gr. ἄψινθος, a bitter-tasting plant. Cf. Jer 9:15 (Heb. 9:14); 23:15; Amos 5:7; Ex 15:23.
Revelation 8:12	“Was struck”: Gr. πλήσσω. Cf. Ex 10:21–23; Joel 2:10; Amos 8:9.
Revelation 8:13	“Angel”: follows the Textus Receptus and some manuscripts (ἀγγέλου; cf. 14:6, another angel “flying in midheaven”); the SBLGNT, codices Sinaiticus and Alexandrinus, and the Byzantine text read “eagle” (ἀετοῦ). “Midheaven”: Gr. μεσουράνημα, the highest point of the sky (cf. 14:6; 19:17). “Woe”: Gr. οὐαί (cf. 9:12; 11:14; 12:12). “Remaining”: Gr. λοιπαί.
Revelation 9:1	“That had fallen”: Gr. πεπτωκότα, perfect participle. “Shaft of the abyss”: Gr. φρέαρ τῆς ἀβύσσου (cf. 11:7; 17:8; 20:1–3).
Revelation 9:2	“Furnace”: cf. Gen 19:28; Ex 19:18. “Were darkened”: cf. Joel 2:10. Variant: the Byzantine text reads “a burning furnace”.
Revelation 9:3	“Locusts”: cf. Ex 10:12–15; Joel 1:4; 2:4–10.
Revelation 9:4	“Green thing”: Gr. χλωρόν, lit. “green” (cf. 8:7). “Except”: Gr. εἰ μή. “The seal of God on the foreheads”: cf. 7:2–3; Ezek 9:4, 6. Variant: the Byzantine text and the Textus Receptus read “on their foreheads”; the Textus Receptus also reads “only the people”.
Revelation 9:5	“Tormented”: Gr. βασανίζω (cf. 14:10; 20:10). “Five months”: cf. 9:10. “Strikes”: Gr. παίω, “to hit”.
Revelation 9:6	“By no means”: Gr. οὐ μή; the Textus Receptus reads “will not find”. “Flees”: present in the Greek; the Byzantine text and the Textus Receptus read “will flee”. Cf. Job 3:21; Jer 8:3.
Revelation 9:7	“Forms”: Gr. ὁμοιώματα, lit. “likenesses”. “Horses prepared for war”: cf. Joel 2:4–5; Job 39:19–25. “Something like crowns”: Gr. ὡς στέφανοι.
Revelation 9:8	“Teeth… of lions”: cf. Joel 1:6.
Revelation 9:9	“Sound”: Gr. φωνή, “voice”. Cf. Joel 2:5.
Revelation 9:10	“Stings”: Gr. κέντρα. Variant: the Textus Receptus reads “and there were stings in their tails”; the Byzantine text, “and they have authority to harm”.
Revelation 9:11	“Abaddon”: Heb. אֲבַדּוֹן‎, “destruction” (cf. Job 26:6; 28:22; Ps 88:11 (Heb. 88:12); Prov 15:11). “Apollyon”: Gr. Ἀπολλύων, “destroyer”.
Revelation 9:12	“Woe”: Gr. οὐαί (cf. 8:13; 11:14).
Revelation 9:13	“A voice”: Gr. φωνὴν μίαν, lit. “a single voice”. “Horns of the golden altar”: cf. Ex 30:1–3; Rev 8:3. “Four”: Gr. τεσσάρων, following the Byzantine text and the Textus Receptus; the SBLGNT follows Papyrus 47 and Codex Alexandrinus, which omit “four”.
Revelation 9:14	“Release”: Gr. λῦσον. “Bound”: Gr. δεδεμένους, perfect participle. “The great river Euphrates”: cf. 16:12; Gen 15:18; Isa 8:7.
Revelation 9:15	“For the hour, and day, and month, and year”: a single article for the four terms, which may indicate an appointed moment. Alternative reading: a period formed by adding the four terms together (cf. Num 14:34; Ezek 4:6, a day for a year). Variant: the Byzantine text reads “for the hour and for the day”.
Revelation 9:16	“Two hundred million”: Gr. δισμυριάδες μυριάδων, lit. “two myriads of myriads”, that is, twice ten thousand times ten thousand (cf. 5:11; Dan 7:10). Variant: the Byzantine text reads “myriads of myriads”.
Revelation 9:17	“The color of fire… of jacinth… of sulfur”: Gr. πυρίνους, ὑακινθίνους, θειώδεις, lit. “of fire, of jacinth, of sulfur”; jacinth is a dark-blue or purple stone. “Fire, and smoke, and sulfur”: cf. Gen 19:24, 28; Rev 14:10; 19:20; 21:8.
Revelation 9:18	“Plagues”: Gr. πληγαί (cf. 15:1; 16:9). Variant: the Textus Receptus omits “plagues”.
Revelation 9:19	Variant: the Textus Receptus reads “their authority” in place of “the authority of the horses”.
Revelation 9:20	“Even so did not”: Gr. οὐδέ; the Byzantine text and the Textus Receptus read “did not”. “The rest”: Gr. λοιποί (cf. 19:21; 20:5). “So as not to worship”: Gr. ἵνα μή. “Idols… which can neither see”: cf. Deut 4:28; Ps 115:4–7; 135:15–17; Dan 5:23.
Revelation 9:21	“Sorceries”: Gr. φάρμακα, lit. “drugs, potions”, here in the sense of spells; the Byzantine text and the Textus Receptus read φαρμακειῶν, “sorceries”. “Prostitution”: Gr. πορνεία (cf. 2:21).
Revelation 10:1	“Angel”: the capital identifies this Angel with Christ; the rainbow, the face like the sun and the feet like pillars of fire recall the divine figures of 1:15–16 and 4:3 and the man clothed in linen of Dan 10:5–6; 12:7. Another reading sees here a mighty angel (cf. 5:2; 18:21). “Clothed in a cloud”: Gr. περιβάλλω (cf. 3:5). “The rainbow”: Gr. ἡ ἶρις (cf. 4:3). “His face like the sun”: cf. 1:16. “Pillars of fire”: cf. Ex 13:21. Variant: the Byzantine text omits “another”.
Revelation 10:2	“Little book”: Gr. βιβλαρίδιον, a diminutive of βιβλίον, “book” (cf. 5:1; 10:8); the Byzantine text reads βιβλίον. “Open”: Gr. ἠνεῳγμένον, perfect participle.
Revelation 10:3	“As a lion roars”: Gr. μυκᾶται, lit. “bellows” (cf. Hos 11:10; Amos 3:8). “Uttered their voices”: lit. “spoke their own voices”. “The seven thunders”: cf. Ps 29:3–9.
Revelation 10:4	“Seal up”: Gr. σφράγισον (cf. Dan 8:26; 12:4, 9; Rev 22:10).
Revelation 10:5	“Raised His right hand to heaven”: cf. Dan 12:7; Deut 32:40. Variant: the Textus Receptus omits “right”.
Revelation 10:6	“Him who lives forever and ever”: cf. 4:9–10. “Who created heaven… and the sea”: cf. 14:7; Ex 20:11; Neh 9:6. “There will be no more time”: Gr. ὅτι χρόνος οὐκέτι ἔσται, lit. “that time will be no longer”; alternative reading: “there will be no more delay”.
Revelation 10:7	“Will be completed”: Gr. καὶ ἐτελέσθη, aorist, lit. “and was completed”, with a future sense (cf. 15:1); the Textus Receptus reads τελεσθῇ, “should be completed”. “Mystery”: Gr. μυστήριον (cf. 1:20; 17:5, 7). “Announced the good news”: Gr. εὐηγγέλισεν (cf. 14:6; Amos 3:7).
Revelation 10:8	“Book”: here Gr. βιβλίον; the Textus Receptus reads βιβλαρίδιον, and the Byzantine text βιβλιδάριον, both “little book”.
Revelation 10:9	“Devour it”: Gr. κατάφαγε, an intensive form of “eat”. Variant: the Textus Receptus reads “saying to him: Give me the little book”. “Stomach”: Gr. κοιλία. Cf. Ezek 2:8–3:3; Jer 15:16 (Heb.); Ps 119:103.
Revelation 10:10	“Became bitter”: Gr. ἐπικράνθη (cf. 8:11). Variant: the Byzantine text reads βιβλίον, “book”, for “little book”.
Revelation 10:11	“They say to me”: plural in the Greek; the Textus Receptus reads “he says to me”. “About”: Gr. ἐπί with the dative, also “before” (in the presence of) or “against”. “Many”: in the Greek it comes after “kings” (βασιλεῦσιν πολλοῖς), but it can apply to all the terms. “Peoples, and nations, and tongues, and kings”: cf. 5:9; 7:9; 17:15.
Revelation 11:1	“Reed”: Gr. κάλαμος, “reed, cane”, used as a measuring rod; Ezekiel’s was six long cubits, about 3 meters (10 feet) (Ezek 40:3–5; cf. 21:15–16). “Like a rod”: Gr. ὅμοιος ῥάβδῳ; ῥάβδος is “rod, staff”, the same word as in 2:27; 12:5; 19:15 (“rod of iron”). “I was told”: Gr. λέγων, lit. “saying”, with no expressed subject; the Textus Receptus reads “and the angel stood, saying”. “Sanctuary”: Gr. ναός (cf. 3:12; 7:15). “Measure”: cf. Ezek 40–42; Zech 2:1–2 (Heb. 2:5–6).
Revelation 11:2	“Leave it out”: Gr. ἔκβαλε ἔξωθεν, lit. “cast it outside”. “Trample”: cf. Luke 21:24; Dan 8:13. “Forty-two months”: cf. 11:3; 12:6, 14; 13:5; Dan 7:25; 12:7.
Revelation 11:3	“I will grant… to prophesy”: lit. “I will give… and they will prophesy” (cf. 3:21). “Witnesses”: Gr. μάρτυσιν (cf. 1:5; 2:13). “One thousand two hundred and sixty days”: cf. 12:6. “Sackcloth”: Gr. σάκκους (cf. 6:12).
Revelation 11:4	“Olive trees… lampstands”: cf. Zech 4:2–3, 11–14; Rev 1:20. “Lord of the earth”: the Textus Receptus reads “God of the earth”.
Revelation 11:5	“Fire… devours their enemies”: cf. 2 Kgs 1:10–12; Jer 5:14.
Revelation 11:6	“Shut the sky… rain”: cf. 1 Kgs 17:1; Jas 5:17. “Waters… into blood”: cf. Ex 7:17–20. “Plague”: Gr. πληγή (cf. 9:18).
Revelation 11:7	“Completed”: Gr. τελέσωσιν (cf. 10:7). “Beast”: Gr. θηρίον (cf. 13:1; 17:8; Dan 7:3, 7, 21). “Comes up out of the abyss”: cf. 9:1; 17:8.
Revelation 11:8	“Dead body”: Gr. τὸ πτῶμα, singular, also in 11:9a; plural in 11:9b. Variants: the Textus Receptus reads “dead bodies” (also in 11:9a) and “our Lord”. “Will lie”: the Greek has no verb. “Spiritually”: Gr. πνευματικῶς. “Sodom and Egypt”: cf. Isa 1:9–10; Ezek 16:46–49.
Revelation 11:9	“Some from”: Gr. ἐκ τῶν, lit. “from among” (cf. 5:9). “Peoples, and tribes, and tongues, and nations”: cf. 5:9; 7:9; 10:11. “Tomb”: Gr. μνῆμα.
Revelation 11:10	“Tormented”: Gr. βασανίζω (cf. 9:5). Variant: the Byzantine text and the Textus Receptus read “will not permit” (11:9) and “will be glad”; the Textus Receptus also reads “will look” (11:9).
Revelation 11:11	“Breath of life”: Gr. πνεῦμα ζωῆς, “spirit, breath of life” (cf. Gen 2:7, Septuagint πνοή; Ezek 37:5, 10); the Septuagint uses πνεῦμα ζωῆς in Gen 7:15 and Ezek 37:5. “Stood on their feet”: cf. Ezek 37:10.
Revelation 11:12	“Come up here”: cf. 4:1. “In the cloud”: cf. 2 Kgs 2:11; Acts 1:9. Variant: the Byzantine text reads “And I heard”.
Revelation 11:13	“Seven thousand names of men”: Gr. ὀνόματα ἀνθρώπων, that is, persons (cf. 3:4). Variant: the Byzantine text reads “in that day”. “The rest”: Gr. λοιποί (cf. 9:20). “Gave glory”: cf. 14:7; 16:9.
Revelation 11:14	“Soon”: Gr. ταχύ (cf. 2:16; 22:7). “Woe”: cf. 8:13; 9:12.
Revelation 11:15	“Has become”: Gr. ἐγένετο. “His Christ”: Gr. τοῦ χριστοῦ αὐτοῦ, “His Anointed” (cf. Ps 2:2; Dan 2:44; 7:14, 27). Variant: the Textus Receptus reads “the kingdoms of the world have become”.
Revelation 11:16	“Before God”: the Byzantine text reads “before the throne of God”.
Revelation 11:17	“He Who Is, and Who Was”: without “and Who Is Coming” (cf. 1:4, 8; 4:8); the Textus Receptus adds “and Who Is Coming”. “Have taken”: Gr. εἴληφας, perfect. “Have begun to reign”: Gr. ἐβασίλευσας, lit. “You reigned”.
Revelation 11:18	“The nations were enraged”: cf. Ps 2:1–5; 99:1 [LXX 98:1]. “The time for the dead to be judged”: Gr. ὁ καιρὸς τῶν νεκρῶν κριθῆναι; καιρός = “time” (cf. 1:3; Dan 7:10, 22). “Reward”: Gr. μισθός (cf. 22:12). “The small and the great”: cf. 13:16; 19:5; 20:12; Ps 115:13 [LXX 113:21]. “Destroying those who destroy”: Gr. διαφθεῖραι… διαφθείροντας, also “corrupt”.
Revelation 11:19	“Sanctuary… in heaven”: Gr. ναός (cf. 15:5; Heb 8:1–5; 9:24). “Ark of His covenant”: Gr. κιβωτὸς τῆς διαθήκης (cf. Ex 25:10–22; Deut 10:1–5; Heb 9:4). “Flashes of lightning, and voices, and thunders”: cf. 4:5; 8:5; 16:18. Variant: the Byzantine text reads “the ark of the covenant of the Lord” and omits “and an earthquake”.
Revelation 12:1	“Sign”: Gr. σημεῖον (cf. 12:3; 15:1). “Clothed with the sun”: Gr. περιβάλλω (cf. 3:5). “Twelve stars”: cf. Gen 37:9. “Crown”: Gr. στέφανος (cf. 2:10).
Revelation 12:2	“Cries out”: present in the Greek, as in the Textus Receptus; the Byzantine text reads “cried out”. “Labor pains”: Gr. ὠδίνω (cf. Isa 26:17; 66:7–8; Mic 4:10). “In torment”: Gr. βασανίζω (cf. 9:5).
Revelation 12:3	“Red”: Gr. πυρρός, lit. “fire-colored” (cf. 6:4). “Seven heads and ten horns”: cf. 13:1; 17:3, 7; Dan 7:7, 20, 24. “Diadems”: Gr. διαδήματα (cf. 13:1; 19:12).
Revelation 12:4	“Sweeps away”: present in the Greek. “A third of the stars”: cf. Dan 8:10. “Was standing”: Gr. ἕστηκεν, perfect. “Devour”: Gr. καταφάγῃ (cf. 10:9). “Child”: Gr. τέκνον; for the capital, cf. 12:5.
Revelation 12:5	“A Son, a male Child”: Gr. υἱὸν ἄρσεν (cf. Isa 66:7); the capital follows the identification given in the verse itself, the rod of iron of Ps 2:9. “Is about to”: Gr. μέλλει (cf. 1:19). “Shepherd… with a rod of iron”: cf. 2:27; 19:15; Ps 2:9. “Caught up”: Gr. ἡρπάσθη, “seized, snatched away”.
Revelation 12:6	“Wilderness”: Gr. ἔρημος (cf. 12:14; 17:3). “They may nourish”: Gr. τρέφωσιν, an impersonal plural. “One thousand two hundred and sixty days”: cf. 11:3; 12:14; 13:5.
Revelation 12:7	“Michael”: cf. Dan 10:13, 21; 12:1; Jude 9. “His”: the capital identifies Michael with Christ: the “great prince” who stands up over “the sons of your people” (Dan 12:1), the archangel (Jude 9) whose voice accompanies the Lord’s descent (1 Thess 4:16). Another reading sees here a created archangel, distinct from Christ. “Made war”: lit. “Michael and his angels to make war”, an infinitive in the Greek (τοῦ πολεμῆσαι); the Textus Receptus has the finite verb (ἐπολέμησαν).
Revelation 12:8	“He did not prevail”: singular in the Greek (the dragon), with Codex Alexandrinus and the Byzantine text; Codex Sinaiticus and the Textus Receptus read the plural, “they did not prevail”. The Byzantine text also reads “for him” in place of “for them”.
Revelation 12:9	“The ancient serpent”: cf. 20:2; Gen 3:1, 14. “devil”: Gr. διάβολος, “accuser, slanderer”. “Satan”: Heb. “adversary” (cf. Job 1:6; Zech 3:1). “Deceives”: Gr. πλανάω (cf. 2:20). “The whole world”: cf. 3:10.
Revelation 12:10	“Now”: Gr. ἄρτι. “His Christ”: cf. 11:15. “Accuser”: Gr. κατήγωρ (cf. Job 1:9–11; Zech 3:1).
Revelation 12:11	“Because of”: Gr. διά with the accusative. “Testimony”: Gr. μαρτυρία (cf. 6:9). “Life”: Gr. ψυχή (cf. Mark 8:35; John 12:25).
Revelation 12:12	“Rejoice”: Gr. εὐφραίνω (cf. 11:10). “Have your tabernacle”: Gr. σκηνοῦντες, “dwelling in a tent” (cf. 7:15; 13:6), in contrast with “those who dwell on the earth” (κατοικέω). “Woe”: Gr. οὐαί (cf. 8:13). “Fury”: Gr. θυμός (cf. 14:8, 10; 16:19). “Little time”: Gr. ὀλίγον καιρόν. Variant: the Textus Receptus reads “Woe to those who dwell on the earth and the sea”.
Revelation 12:13	“The male Child”: Gr. τὸν ἄρσενα (cf. 12:5).
Revelation 12:14	“Great eagle”: cf. Ex 19:4; Deut 32:11; Isa 40:31. “A time, and times, and half a time”: Gr. καιρὸν καὶ καιροὺς καὶ ἥμισυ καιροῦ (cf. 12:6; Dan 7:25; 12:7). “Away from the face”: Gr. ἀπὸ προσώπου. Variant: the Byzantine text reads “so that she may be nourished”.

Revelation 12:16	“Swallowed”: cf. Num 16:30–32.

Revelation 13:1	“Coming up out of the sea”: cf. Dan 7:3. “Ten horns and seven heads”: cf. 12:3; 17:3, 7, 12; Dan 7:7, 24; the Textus Receptus reverses the order (“seven heads and ten horns”). “Names of blasphemy”: the Textus Receptus reads “name”. Variant: the Byzantine text and the Textus Receptus begin the verse with “And I stood on the sand of the sea” (cf. 12:17).
Revelation 13:2	“Leopard… bear… lion”: cf. Dan 7:4–6. “Power… throne… authority”: Gr. δύναμις, θρόνος, ἐξουσία (cf. 2:13; 12:9).
Revelation 13:3	“And one of its heads was”: lit. “and one of its heads”, with no verb; the Textus Receptus reads “And I saw one of its heads”. “As though slain to death”: Gr. ὡς ἐσφαγμένην εἰς θάνατον, the same participle used for the Lamb (5:6, “as though slain”, in the neuter). “Mortal wound”: Gr. ἡ πληγὴ τοῦ θανάτου, lit. “the wound of its death”; πληγή, also “plague” (cf. 9:18). “Following the beast”: lit. “after the beast”.
Revelation 13:4	“Who is like”: cf. Ex 15:11; Ps 35:10 [LXX 34:10]; 89:6–8 (Heb. 89:7–9) [LXX 88:7–9]; Mic 7:18.

Revelation 21:20	The exact identification of some stones is uncertain. “Sardonyx”: Gr. σαρδόνυξ. “Sardius”: Gr. σάρδιον (cf. 4:3). “Chrysolite”: Gr. χρυσόλιθος, lit. “gold stone”. “Chrysoprase”: Gr. χρυσόπρασος, a golden-green stone. “Jacinth”: Gr. ὑάκινθος (cf. 9:17).
Revelation 21:21	“Pearls”: Gr. μαργαρῖται (cf. 17:4; 18:12, 16; Matt 13:45–46). “Street”: Gr. πλατεῖα, the main street (cf. 11:8; 22:2). “Transparent glass”: Gr. ὕαλος διαυγής; the Textus Receptus reads διαφανής.
Revelation 21:22	“Sanctuary”: Gr. ναός (cf. 3:12; 7:15; 11:19). The sanctuary of the city is the presence of God and of the Lamb (cf. 21:3; 22:3–4; John 2:19–21), and the city itself has the cubic form of the Most Holy Place (cf. 21:16; 1 Kgs 6:20). Alternative reading: the verse stresses the primacy of the divine presence, before which any structure becomes secondary. “The Lord God, the Almighty”: cf. 1:8; 4:8; 11:17; 15:3.
Revelation 21:23	“No need of the sun, nor of the moon”: cf. 22:5; Isa 60:19–20. “Has illuminated it”: Gr. ἐφώτισεν, aorist. “Its lamp is the Lamb”: Gr. λύχνος (cf. 18:23; 22:5; John 8:12).
Revelation 21:24	“The nations will walk by its light”: Gr. διὰ τοῦ φωτὸς αὐτῆς, lit. “through its light” (cf. Isa 60:3, 5, 11). “Their glory”: Gr. αὐτῶν, the glory of the kings. “Bring”: present in the Greek. Variants: the Byzantine text reads “bring to it glory and honor of the nations”; the Textus Receptus reads “the nations of those who are saved will walk in its light” and “their glory and honor”.
Revelation 21:25	“Will never be shut by day”: Gr. οὐ μὴ κλεισθῶσιν (cf. Isa 60:11; Zech 14:7). “There will be no night”: cf. 22:5.
Revelation 21:26	“The glory and the honor of the nations”: cf. Isa 60:5, 11; 66:12.
Revelation 21:27	“Nothing profane”: Gr. πᾶν κοινόν, “everything common, profane”; the Textus Receptus reads κοινοῦν, “that which defiles”. “Abomination and falsehood”: cf. 21:8; 22:15. “Written in the book of life of the Lamb”: cf. 3:5; 13:8; 17:8; 20:12, 15; Isa 52:1; Ezek 44:9.
Revelation 22:1	“River of the water of life”: cf. 7:17; 21:6; Ezek 47:1–12; Zech 14:8; Ps 46:4 (Heb. 46:5) [LXX 45:5]; John 7:38. “Bright”: Gr. λαμπρός (cf. 15:6; 19:8). “Coming out”: Gr. ἐκπορεύομαι. Variant: the Byzantine text and the Textus Receptus read “a pure river of the water of life”.
Revelation 22:2	“Street”: Gr. πλατεῖα (cf. 21:21). “On either side of the river”: Gr. ἐντεῦθεν καὶ ἐκεῖθεν (cf. Ezek 47:7, 12). “The tree of life”: Gr. ξύλον ζωῆς, possibly collective, “trees” (cf. 2:7; 22:14, 19; Gen 2:9; 3:22–24). “Every month”: lit. “according to each month”. “Healing”: Gr. θεραπεία (cf. Ezek 47:12).
Revelation 22:3	“Curse”: Gr. κατάθεμα, “accursed thing” (cf. Gen 3:17; Zech 14:11); the Textus Receptus reads κατανάθεμα. “The throne of God and of the Lamb”: cf. 3:21; 7:17. “His servants will serve Him”: Gr. αὐτοῦ… αὐτῷ, singular, referring to God and the Lamb together (cf. 11:15; 20:6). “Serve”: Gr. λατρεύω, to serve in worship (cf. 7:15).
Revelation 22:4	“They will see His face”: cf. Ex 33:20, 23; Ps 17:15 [LXX 16:15]; Matt 5:8; 1 Cor 13:12; 1 John 3:2. “His name… on their foreheads”: cf. 3:12; 7:3; 14:1, in contrast with 13:16–17.
Revelation 22:5	“There will be no more night”: cf. 21:25; Zech 14:7. “Have no need”: present in the Greek (cf. 21:23). “Will shine on them”: Gr. φωτίσει ἐπ’ αὐτούς, lit. “will give light upon them” (cf. Isa 60:19–20; Num 6:25). “Will reign”: cf. 5:10; 20:4, 6; Dan 7:18, 27. Variant: the Byzantine text and the Textus Receptus read “there will be no night there” and “need of a lamp”; the Textus Receptus reads “gives them light”.
Revelation 22:6	“Faithful and true”: cf. 19:9; 21:5. “The God of the spirits of the prophets”: cf. Num 27:16; 1 Cor 14:32; the Textus Receptus reads “the God of the holy prophets”; the Byzantine text reads “And he says to me”. “To show His servants the things that must soon happen”: takes up 1:1 and frames the book.
Revelation 22:7	“Behold, I am coming soon”: cf. 2:16; 3:11; 22:12, 20. “Blessed”: cf. 1:3; 14:13; 16:15; 19:9; 20:6; 22:14, the sixth of the seven beatitudes of the book. “Keeps”: Gr. τηρέω (cf. 1:3; 3:10; 12:17; 14:12).
Revelation 22:8	“Am the one who heard and saw”: Gr. ὁ ἀκούων καὶ βλέπων, present participles, lit. “the one who hears and sees” (cf. 1:1, 4, 9). “I fell down to worship”: cf. 19:10.
Revelation 22:9	“See that you do not do that”: Gr. ὅρα μή (cf. 19:10). “Fellow servant”: Gr. σύνδουλος (cf. 6:11; 19:10). “The prophets, and of those who keep the words of this book”: cf. 19:10. “Worship God”: cf. 14:7.
Revelation 22:10	“Do not seal up”: in contrast with Dan 8:26; 12:4, 9 (cf. 10:4). “The time is near”: Gr. ὁ καιρὸς ἐγγύς (cf. 1:3).
Revelation 22:11	“The one who does wrong”: Gr. ὁ ἀδικῶν, “the one who practices injustice”; the same verb is “harm” in 2:11; 7:2–3. “Still”: Gr. ἔτι, “let him continue”. Cf. Dan 12:10; Ezek 3:27. “Be made holy”: Gr. ἁγιασθήτω (cf. Lev 11:44). Variant: the Textus Receptus reads “and the righteous, let him be made righteous still”.
Revelation 22:12	“Reward”: Gr. μισθός (cf. 11:18; Isa 40:10; 62:11). “To repay each one according to his work”: lit. “as his work is” (cf. 2:23; 20:12–13; Ps 62:12 (Heb. 62:13) [LXX 61:13]; Prov 24:12; Matt 16:27; Rom 2:6). Variant: the Byzantine text and the Textus Receptus read “as his work will be”.
Revelation 22:13	“The Alpha and the Omega”: cf. 1:8; 21:6. “The First and the Last”: cf. 1:17; 2:8; Isa 44:6; 48:12. “The Beginning and the End”: cf. 3:14; 21:6. Titles of God in 1:8 and 21:6, here on the lips of Jesus (cf. 22:16). Variant: the Textus Receptus reads “the Beginning and the End, the First and the Last”.
Revelation 22:14	“Blessed”: the seventh of the seven beatitudes of the book (cf. 1:3; 14:13; 16:15; 19:9; 20:6; 22:7). “Those who wash their robes”: Gr. οἱ πλύνοντες τὰς στολὰς αὐτῶν (cf. 7:14; 3:4–5; 19:8); the reading of Codex Sinaiticus and Codex Alexandrinus. Variant: the Byzantine text and the Textus Receptus read “those who do His commandments”, Gr. οἱ ποιοῦντες τὰς ἐντολὰς αὐτοῦ (cf. 12:17; 14:12; John 14:15; 1 John 5:3). In Greek capital letters the two readings look very much alike. “Right”: Gr. ἐξουσία, “authority, right” (cf. 2:7; 22:2, 19). “By the gates”: cf. 21:12, 25, 27; Ps 118:19–20 [LXX 117:19–20]; Gen 3:24.
Revelation 22:15	“Outside”: cf. 21:8, 27; Matt 8:12. “Dogs”: an image of the impure (cf. Deut 23:18 (Heb. 23:19); Phil 3:2). “Sorcerers… immoral… murderers… idolaters”: cf. 21:8. “Who loves and practices falsehood”: cf. 14:5; 21:27; 2 Thess 2:10–12.
Revelation 22:16	“I, Jesus”: cf. 1:1. “For the churches”: Gr. ἐπὶ ταῖς ἐκκλησίαις, also “to the churches” or “concerning the churches”. “The Root and the Offspring of David”: cf. 5:5; Isa 11:1, 10; Rom 1:3. “The bright Morning Star”: cf. 2:28; Num 24:17; 2 Pet 1:19. Variant: the Textus Receptus reads “the bright and morning Star”.
Revelation 22:17	“The Spirit”: cf. 2:7; 14:13. “The bride”: cf. 21:2, 9. “Come!”: Gr. ἔρχου (cf. 22:20). “The one who is thirsty… freely”: cf. 21:6; Isa 55:1; John 7:37. Variant: the Textus Receptus reads “and whoever wishes”.
Revelation 22:18	“I testify”: Gr. μαρτυρῶ (cf. 1:2); the Textus Receptus reads “For I testify together”. “If anyone adds to them”: cf. Deut 4:2; 12:32 (Heb. 13:1); Prov 30:6 [LXX 24:29]. “The plagues”: cf. 15:1, 6; 16:1–21; Deut 29:20 (Heb. 29:19). Variant: the Byzantine text reads “may God add” (and, in v. 19, “may God take away”).
Revelation 22:19	“Takes away”: Gr. ἀφαιρέω (cf. Deut 4:2). “His part from the tree of life”: cf. 2:7; 22:2, 14. Variant: the Textus Receptus reads “from the book of life”, a reading without support in the ancient Greek manuscripts (cf. 3:5; 20:12).
Revelation 22:20	“He who testifies”: cf. 1:2, 5; 3:14. “Yes”: Gr. ναί (cf. 1:7; 14:13; 16:7). “Come, Lord Jesus”: Gr. ἔρχου, κύριε Ἰησοῦ, the Greek equivalent of the Aramaic Maranatha (cf. 1 Cor 16:22). Variant: the Byzantine text and the Textus Receptus read “Amen. Yes, come, Lord Jesus”.
Revelation 22:21	“The grace… be with all”: cf. 1:4; 2 Thess 3:18. “Amen”: with Codex Sinaiticus, the Byzantine text and the Textus Receptus; Codex Alexandrinus omits it. Variants: Codex Sinaiticus reads “with the saints”; the Byzantine text reads “of the Lord Jesus Christ with all the saints”; the Textus Receptus reads “of our Lord Jesus Christ with you all”.
