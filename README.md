## Why the VBT exists

Scripture should be freely available to everyone called to write a book, a devotional, a commentary, or anything that will bless others. The VBT began with a devotional on Revelation. Quoting all 404 verses of the book from an existing translation went beyond what its license allowed. Going on meant paying a fee and accepting limits on how far the material could travel. English has free, high-quality translations. Portuguese had none translated from today's critical Greek and Hebrew texts that was faithful, fluent and truly free.

That wall became a calling. The Veni Bible Translation exists so that anyone, whether a pastor preparing a study, a youth leader printing a handout, or a family reading at the table, can hold the Word in their own language, as close to the original as possible, without asking permission. The name comes from the Bible's last prayer: *Veni, Domine Iesu.* **Come, Lord Jesus.**

Every verse is translated from the Greek of the SBL Greek New Testament, and each decision is shown in the notes for anyone to check. Revelation is now complete in Portuguese and English, with 404 verses and 387 notes in each. The rest of Scripture will follow, book by book. And it is, and always will be, free for anyone, anywhere.

<sub>*Project led by Ismael Silva, who holds degrees in theology, has studied Koine Greek and Hebrew, and has more than 20 years of pastoral ministry on two continents.*</sub>

## Translation principles

- **Faithful.** A formal-equivalence translation of the original text, in the family of the ESV, NKJV and BSB. No content is added or removed; where the target language needs a word the Greek leaves implicit, the note says so.
- **Fluent.** Natural Brazilian Portuguese and English. The narrative "and" chains of the Greek are kept where they carry meaning (visions, series, lists, hymns) and lightened where they only slow the reader.
- **One translation, two languages.** The English is translated from the Greek, following the same decisions as the Portuguese verse by verse; the two editions carry the same notes.
- **Consistent.** Each key Greek term has one fixed rendering across the book, checked against a glossary — *santuário* / *sanctuary* for ναός, *tabernáculo* / *tabernacle* for σκηνή, *besta* / *beast* for θηρίον, *testemunho* / *testimony* for μαρτυρία.
- **Transparent.** 387 footnotes in each language show word choices, literal readings, textual variants (Sinaiticus, Alexandrinus, Byzantine text, Textus Receptus), alternative readings and Old Testament allusions, with Hebrew and Septuagint numbering where they differ.
- **Reverent.** Capital letters for the pronouns and titles of God, Christ and the Holy Spirit.
- **Traditional numbering.** 404 verses in Revelation, as in the ARA, NIV and BSB.

**Base texts.** New Testament: SBL Greek New Testament (SBLGNT). Old Testament: Westminster Leningrad Codex (WLC). Where the VBT departs from the base text, the note gives both readings and their witnesses.

## Formats

Each language folder (`pt-BR/`, `en/`) has the same four formats:

| Format | File | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Markdown | `revelation/markdown/` — [pt-BR](pt-BR/revelation/markdown/README.md) · [English](en/revelation/markdown/README.md) | Reading on GitHub, chapter by chapter, with notes |
| USFM 3.0 | `revelation/usfm/67-REV.usfm` — [pt-BR](pt-BR/revelation/usfm/67-REV.usfm) · [English](en/revelation/usfm/67-REV.usfm) | Bible software and publishing (Paratext, eBible, Bible apps); includes headings, poetry and footnotes |
| JSON | `revelation/json/revelation.json` — [pt-BR](pt-BR/revelation/json/revelation.json) · [English](en/revelation/json/revelation.json) | Developers and apps — chapters, headings, verses, notes and layout blocks (paragraphs and poetry lines) |
| Plain text | `revelation/txt/` — [pt-BR](pt-BR/revelation/txt/revelation.txt) · [English](en/revelation/txt/revelation.txt) | Search, quoting and quick reuse — one verse per line; notes in `revelation-notes.txt` |

## Corrections and suggestions

Found a typo or have a suggestion about a rendering? Please [open an issue](https://github.com/ismaelthesilva/veni-bible/issues) with the reference (for example, *Ap 13:3* or *Rev 13:3*) and your reasoning. Every change to the text is checked against the Greek, the glossary and the translation's decision log before a new version is released.
