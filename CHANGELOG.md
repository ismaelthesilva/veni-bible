# Changelog

All notable changes to the VBT text. Versions follow the translation's working editions; the first frozen release will be **VBT Revelation 1.0**.

## v27 — Revelation, pt-BR and English
- English: "mighty" for ἰσχυρός throughout — 5:2, 10:1 and 18:21 "mighty angel", 18:8 "mighty is the Lord God", 18:10 "the mighty city" (already in 18:2; 19:6; 6:15; 19:18). Notes on 5:2, 18:8 and 18:21 aligned.
- pt-BR: "forte" kept in the text; 10:1 note aligned ("um anjo forte").

## v26 — Revelation, pt-BR and English
- 5:1 note: βιβλίον explained as a scroll written on both sides and sealed along its edge, so that each seal opened in turn; "book" / "livro" is kept throughout for the one Greek word (cf. 1:11; 6:14; 22:7). Text unchanged.

## v25 — Revelation, pt-BR and English
- **English edition complete:** all 404 verses and 387 notes, translated from the SBLGNT and following the same decisions as the pt-BR text, verse by verse.
- **pt-BR text:**
  - 10:11 "sobre muitos povos, e nações, e línguas, e reis", with "muitos" placed at the head of the series.
  - 11:9 "Alguns dentre os povos…" (gr. ἐκ τῶν, partitive, with no noun in the Greek).
  - "diabo" in lowercase (2:10; 12:9, 12; 20:2, 10), as a description (διάβολος, "accuser"); "Satanás" remains a proper name.
- **pt-BR notes revised and expanded,** in step with the English notes:
  - Old Testament references now show Hebrew numbering in parentheses and Septuagint numbering in brackets where they differ, e.g. Sl 75:8 (heb. 75:9) [LXX 74:9].
  - Byzantine-text and Textus Receptus variants were checked against the Greek, corrected where needed and added where missing.
  - Every quoted phrase in a note matches the wording of the text.
  - Measures are given in both systems.
- **English decisions** (pt-BR unchanged where already equivalent):
  - 1:1 semicolon, as in the Greek and pt-BR.
  - 3:17 "it is you who are wretched…".
  - 3:18 and 15:4 "made manifest" (φανερόω).
  - 17:8 and 17:11 "destruction" (ἀπώλεια).
  - 19:7 and 19:9 "wedding" (γάμος).

## v24.4 — Revelation (pt-BR), text complete
- 17:17 "o reino deles" (αὐτῶν), with a note distinguishing God's purpose from the kings' kingdom.
- 18:6 "dai-lhe o dobro"; 18:21 "será mais achada"; 22:12 "conforme a sua obra" (literal forms kept in the notes).

## v24.3
- 6:5 and 6:12 "preto" for μέλας; 6:14 "pergaminho" (the rolled-up scroll), with a note.

## v24.2
- 2:23 "com peste" for ἐν θανάτῳ (a Hebrew idiom for pestilence; cf. Ez 33:27 LXX).
- Traditional numbering: the sentence of 12:18 in the critical editions closes 12:17. Revelation has 404 verses.

## v24
- Connective style applied across the book: narrative verse-initial "E" reduced from 228 to 125 while visions, series, lists and hymns keep their "and" chains. Colons after "tocou a trombeta" and "derramou a sua taça".

## v23
- Book-wide consistency review: notes aligned with the final wording, καθήμενος always "sentado", glossary complete.

## v1 – v22
- Revelation translated chapter by chapter from the SBLGNT, with footnotes on word choices, literal readings, textual variants, alternative readings and Old Testament allusions.
